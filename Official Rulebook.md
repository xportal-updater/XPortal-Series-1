# Organizer

The **XP Game Fest 2026 – Counter-Strike 2 Tournament** is organized by **XPortal Esports (GG Esports)**, a Kosovan company with Unique Registration Code **(CUI) RKS 810874281**, legally represented by **Besnik Thaqi, CEO**.

This tournament is officially sanctioned as a **Tier 2 Valve Regional Standings (VRS) Ranked Event**. Results will count toward **Valve’s official regional standings**.

XPortal adheres to Valve’s rules for ranked tournaments, ensuring a fair structure for invites and qualifiers.

The **Valve Regional Standings (VRS)** is the official ranking system used by Valve to gauge team performance and determine invitations for CS2 events.

This rulebook outlines all regulations, format details, and expectations for teams participating in **XP Game Fest 2026**.

> All time references in this rulebook are in **Prishtina local time (GMT+1)**.

# GSL Match Flow

### Opening Matches
- Seed #1 vs Seed #15 (Bo3)
- Seed #6 vs Seed #10 (Bo3)


### Winners Match

Winner Match 1 vs Winner Match 2  
Winner advances to playoffs.

### Elimination Match

Loser Match 1 vs Loser Match 2  
Loser eliminated.

### Decider Match

Loser Winners Match vs Winner Elimination Match  
Winner advances.

Result per group:

- **2 teams advance**
- **2 teams eliminated**

# Map Veto Process

## Best-of-1

A ban  
B ban  
A ban  
B ban  
A ban  
B ban  
Last map played  

Starting side decided by **knife round**.

---

## Best-of-3

A ban  
B ban  
A pick  
B choose side  
B pick  
A choose side  
B ban  
A ban  
Remaining map = Map 3  

Knife round decides sides on **Map 3**.
